# Provider brand marks

These files show which service or source a song comes from. Every mark is a trademark of its owner and is shown only to identify that service. Each brand folder holds `color` (full-colour mark) and `glyph` (single-colour mark) in SVG where a vector exists, plus 512 px PNG masters. Marks were re-centred on a square canvas with consistent padding; their artwork was not otherwise changed, except where noted.
